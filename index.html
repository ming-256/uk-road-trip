Waterfall Country

WaterfallsBrecon Beacons · 2-4 hrs · ⭐ 4.9

A series of stunning waterfalls in the Brecon Beacons. At their BEST in autumn rain — powerful, dramatic, unforgettable.

Wear waterproofs! Paths can be slippery. November is peak flow.

🚗 5 min
2

Sgwd yr Eira (Walk Behind!)

WaterfallsBrecon Beacons · 1-2 hrs · ⭐ 5

Walk BEHIND a waterfall. The path goes directly behind the curtain of water. One of the most magical experiences in Wales.

You WILL get wet. Bring waterproofs and a sense of adventure.

🚗 5 min
3

Four Falls Walk

WaterfallsBrecon Beacons · 3-4 hrs · ⭐ 4.8

A 4-5 mile walk past four major waterfalls. Each one is different and spectacular.

The best waterfall walk in Wales. Start from Ystradfellte car park.

🚗 5 min
4

Sgwd Ddwli (Waterfall)

WaterfallsBrecon Beacons · 1-2 hrs · ⭐ 4.6

A wide, powerful waterfall in Waterfall Country. Less visited than Sgwd yr Eira but equally impressive.

A good option if you want a waterfall without the longer Four Falls walk.

🚗 35 min
5

The Old Black Lion

Pubs & FoodBlack Mountains · 2-3 hrs · ⭐ 4.6

17th-century coaching inn in Hay. Candlelit dining room, log fires. The most romantic dinner spot in Hay.

Book for dinner. Try the Welsh lamb.
